Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 February 2026

Wine Selector ("the App") is designed with privacy as a core principle. The App operates entirely on your device with no data collection, no accounts, and no internet required for its core functionality.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. Specifically:

  • No personal data is collected or stored
  • No analytics, tracking, or telemetry data is gathered
  • No account or registration is required
  • No data is sent to any server

Camera Permission

The App requests camera access solely to photograph wine lists for on-device text recognition. Photos are:

  • Processed entirely on your device
  • Stored temporarily in the App's private cache directory
  • Never uploaded, shared, or transmitted to any server
  • Automatically overwritten each time you take a new photo

On-Device Processing

All text recognition and wine pairing analysis happens on your device using Google ML Kit's on-device text recognition library. This library:

  • Runs entirely on your device
  • Does not send image data to Google's servers
  • Does not require an internet connection

For more information, see Google ML Kit's terms and privacy information.

Data Storage

The App stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Preferences — Your price limit, wine type filters, and grape preferences
  • Cached photos — The most recent wine list photo, stored in the App's cache directory

You can clear all App data at any time through Android Settings > Apps > Wine Selector > Storage > Clear Data.

Internet Access

The App does not require an internet connection for its core functionality. No network requests are made during normal use.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party library:

  • Google ML Kit Text Recognition — For on-device optical character recognition (OCR). This library processes images locally and does not transmit data to external servers.

The App does not include any advertising SDKs, analytics services, or crash reporting tools.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. The App contains no user-generated content, no social features, and no data collection mechanisms.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

Contact
