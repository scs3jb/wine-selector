APP NAME (30 char max):
Wine Selector

SHORT DESCRIPTION (80 char max):
Photograph a wine list, pick your food, and get instant pairing recommendations.

FULL DESCRIPTION (4000 char max):
Wine Selector takes the guesswork out of choosing wine at a restaurant.

Simply snap a photo of the wine list, select what you're eating, and get instant, personalised recommendations ranked by how well each wine pairs with your food.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Choose your food - Pick from 12 categories including beef, chicken, fish, seafood, pasta, pizza, and more
2. Photograph the wine list - Use the built-in camera to capture any wine menu
3. Get recommendations - Wines are identified, scored, and ranked by food pairing strength

WINE PAIRING KNOWLEDGE
Built-in profiles for 60+ grape varieties and wine regions, including:
- Red grapes: Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot, Pinot Noir, Malbec, Syrah, Tempranillo, Sangiovese, Nebbiolo, Grenache, and more
- White grapes: Chardonnay, Sauvignon Blanc, Riesling, Pinot Grigio, and more
- Regions: Bordeaux, Burgundy, Rioja, Chianti, Barolo, Barbaresco, Chablis, Sancerre, and more
- Styles: Champagne, Prosecco, Port, Rosé, and more

Each wine gets a pairing score from 1-10 with a clear explanation of why it works with your food.

FEATURES
- Works completely offline - no internet connection required
- No account needed, no data collected
- On-device text recognition (Google ML Kit)
- Wine type filtering (red, white, rosé)
- Price-aware recommendations with budget setting
- Smart OCR handles two-column menus, prices in multiple formats, and accented text
- Clean display names showing producer and wine type

PERFECT FOR
- Restaurant diners who want better wine choices
- Wine beginners who want to learn about food pairings
- Anyone tired of defaulting to "house red"

No sommelier required. Just point, shoot, and choose.

Privacy Policy for Wine Selector

Last updated: 25 February 2026

Wine Selector is designed with privacy in mind. The app operates entirely on your device with no data collection.

Information We Collect:
We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. Specifically:
- Photos taken within the app are stored temporarily in the app's cache directory and are not uploaded anywhere
- No analytics, tracking, or telemetry data is collected
- No account or registration is required
- No internet connection is required for the app's core functionality

Camera Permission:
The app requests camera access solely to photograph wine lists for text recognition. Photos are processed entirely on your device using Google ML Kit's on-device text recognition. Images are not transmitted to any server.

Data Storage:
All app data (preferences, cached photos) is stored locally on your device. You can clear this data at any time through Android Settings > Apps > Wine Selector > Storage > Clear Data.

Third-Party Services:
The app uses Google ML Kit for on-device text recognition. This library runs entirely on your device and does not send image data to Google's servers. See Google's ML Kit privacy information at: https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms

Children's Privacy:
The app does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy:
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.

Contact:
For questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue at the project's GitHub repository.
